What do these numbers mean?
Blood pressure
Normal below 120/80 mmHg · elevated 120–129/<80 · Stage 1 130–139 or 80–89 · Stage 2 140/90 or above · crisis 180/120 or above.
SpO₂ (blood oxygen)
95% and above is normal · 90–94% is low · below 90% is critical — proceed to the health station.
Temperature
37.9 °C or above counts as a fever. Rest, hydrate, and report to your health worker if it persists.
Pulse rate
A typical resting pulse for adults is 60–100 bpm.
BMI
Underweight below 18.5 · normal 18.5–24.9 · overweight 25–29.9 · obese 30 and above.
Weight & height
Measured at the barangay kiosk and used to work out your BMI.
