Operating Architecture

From kiosk interaction to
municipal health network.

Review Audit Trail →
01 Screening

Citizen walk-in or guest triage

The resident arrives at the barangay hall. They scan their citizen QR or authenticate via biometric/username on the touchscreen kiosk. Guest mode is supported for transient individuals.

02 Offline Edge

Local capture & edge computation

The Raspberry Pi controller samples sensors, computes BMI and vital classifications, stores the record locally in SQLite, and displays immediate clinical guidance even without internet.

03 Encrypted Sync

REST API synchronization

When network connectivity is established, the kiosk sends an HMAC-authenticated HTTP POST to the Flask central server, instantly updating personal records and anonymous community totals.

Got Questions?

Answers to common clinical
& technical inquiries.

Review Consent Terms →
How does the kiosk handle power or internet outages?
The kiosk runs an offline-first architecture powered by SQLite on the Raspberry Pi. When power drops, a UPS provides safe shutdown. When internet disconnects, all readings continue to be saved locally with timestamped telemetry. Once connection resumes, readings sync automatically to the central web platform.
Can Barangay Health Workers view my medical records without my permission?
No. In strict compliance with Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), individual medical records are accessible to health workers only when the citizen explicitly grants consent. Citizens can grant or revoke consent at any time from their personal portal.
What physiological parameters does the kiosk measure?
The automated station measures systolic and diastolic blood pressure, SpO₂ blood oxygen saturation, body temperature, pulse rate, weight, and height. It automatically derives Body Mass Index (BMI) and provides instant clinical stage classifications.
Can transient visitors or non-residents use the kiosk?
Yes. The kiosk includes a dedicated Guest Mode. Visitors receive their immediate on-screen vital screening and a printed or QR-based summary, while their anonymized metrics contribute to community epidemiological statistics.
How is the BHW community board kept anonymous?
The BHW Dashboard displays only aggregated statistical counters (e.g. fever rates, hypertension distribution) across the barangay. Individual names, usernames, and record cards are strictly isolated and never rendered on the community operations board.
How are clinical referrals to the Rural Health Unit handled?
When a citizen's vitals demonstrate critical flags (such as Stage 2 Hypertension or severe SpO₂ drops), health workers with authorized consent can generate a formalized, tamper-evident RHU referral package complete with historical trend charts.
